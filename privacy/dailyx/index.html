DailyX — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Who we are

DailyX is a daily puzzle game published by Forgenine (the “developer”, “we”). This policy explains what data is handled when you use the app and your choices. Contact: supportforge9@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

We do not ask for your name, e-mail or any account of our own. Your progress, streaks, stars, level choices and settings are stored only on your device.

3. Google Play Games (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, Google processes your Play Games profile (player name, avatar and player ID) and may store your progress as a saved game in your Google account. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in.

4. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the advertising ID, device and app information, IP address (used for approximate location), ad interactions and diagnostic data to serve, measure and protect ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Learn more: how Google uses data.

5. Purposes and legal bases

Data is used to run the game, keep your progress, show and measure ads and prevent fraud. Legal bases: performance of the service, our legitimate interests and, where required, your consent (Brazilian LGPD, EU/UK GDPR and equivalent laws).

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you clear the app data or uninstall it. Data held by Google (Play Games, AdMob) follows Google's retention rules and can be managed in your Google account.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability or information about the use of your data, and withdraw consent. Write to supportforge9@gmail.com; we will reply within the legal deadlines. You may also contact your data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD).

9. Security

Data exchanged with third-party services is encrypted in transit. No method is 100% secure, but we use reasonable measures to protect it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; relevant changes will be announced in the app or store listing.

Os dados locais ficam no seu aparelho até você limpar os dados do app ou desinstalá-lo. Os dados mantidos pelo Google (Play Games, AdMob) seguem as regras de retenção do Google e podem ser gerenciados na sua conta Google.

Você pode solicitar confirmação, acesso, correção, eliminação, portabilidade e informações sobre o uso dos seus dados, além de revogar o consentimento (art. 18 da LGPD). Escreva para supportforge9@gmail.com; responderemos nos prazos legais. Você também pode recorrer à ANPD.

9. Segurança

Os dados trocados com serviços de terceiros são criptografados em trânsito. Nenhum método é 100% seguro, mas adotamos medidas razoáveis de proteção.

10. Alterações

Podemos atualizar esta política. A data no topo indica a versão mais recente; mudanças relevantes serão informadas no app ou na página da loja.
